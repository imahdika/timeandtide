# Fishing Time & Tide

Bahrain fishing planner for Mina Salman, in English and Arabic.

- Tide curve, highs and lows for any date (official BMD times, then predicted, then a harmonic estimate)
- Best fishing windows scored on tide range, light, wind and moon, plus a 7-day view
- Fish calendar from the chart "جودة أسماك البحرين حسب الشهور الميلادية" by Captain Ahmed bin Isa Al-Busmait (2020)
- SCE seasonal bans, minimum legal sizes and fishing news
- Species guide: depth zone, method, bait and typical weight
